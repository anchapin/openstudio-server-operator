# ADR-4: ValidatingAdmissionPolicies for label/name-scoped verbs

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

The operator's RBAC needs two grants that are broader than the operator's
actual behavior: `pods: [delete]` (worker eviction during recycling) and
`batch/jobs: [create, update, delete]` (the archival Jobs spawned by the
retention pipeline). RBAC `PolicyRule` has **no labelSelector** — a Role
cannot express "delete only pods labeled `app=worker`" or "create only Jobs
that are archival Jobs". The RBAC grant must therefore cover the verb for
*all* pods/jobs in the namespace.

The admission layer can express what RBAC cannot. Labels alone are not
sufficient either: the constrained service account could label any object it
creates with the blessed labels, so a second factor is needed — the
`oscm-archive-*` name pattern generated by `archival.py::archival_job_name`,
which the policy also matches.

## Decision

Narrow the operator and prune service accounts beyond RBAC with
cluster-scoped `ValidatingAdmissionPolicy` + `ValidatingAdmissionPolicyBinding`:

- `deploy/pod-delete-admission-policy.yaml` constrains the operator SA's
  `pods/delete` to pods carrying `app=worker` (the recycling/eviction path).
- `deploy/storage-cronjob.yaml` embeds a second VAP constraining the prune
  SA's `batch/jobs` verbs to Jobs labeled
  `app.kubernetes.io/managed-by=openstudio-operator` AND
  `app.kubernetes.io/component=archival` AND named `oscm-archive-*`.

Humans via `kubectl` and the prune CronJob SA remain unrestricted — only the
operator/prune SAs are narrowed.

## Consequences

- Requires Kubernetes **1.30+** (`admissionregistration.k8s.io/v1` GA);
  pre-1.30 clusters must skip both VAPs and accept RBAC-only breadth.
- Two enforcement layers must stay in sync: when the operator's mutation
  surface changes, RBAC, the VAP match conditions, and the audit-policy
  recipe all move together.
- CEL matchConditions are one more review surface; a policy bug fails
  *closed* (the verb is denied), which is the safe direction.
- The audit trail (`docs/audit-policy.md`) records pods-delete and
  jobs-create/delete at `RequestResponse`, so VAP-narrowed mutations remain
  attributable.
